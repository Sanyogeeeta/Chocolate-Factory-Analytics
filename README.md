# Chocolate Factory Analytics – Power BI Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

Chocolate Factory Analytics is an interactive Power BI dashboard developed to analyze sales, shipments, products, categories, and regional performance of a chocolate business.

The dashboard helps understand overall business performance and identify differences in sales and shipments across products, categories, and geographical regions.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Analyze total sales and shipment performance.
* Compare sales across different products.
* Analyze sales contribution by product category.
* Understand shipment distribution across different countries.
* Compare regional sales performance.
* Track year-over-year (YoY) performance.
* Create an interactive dashboard for business reporting.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* Power BI
* Power Query
* DAX
* Microsoft Excel
* Data Visualization
* Data Cleaning & Transformation

## 📌 Key Dashboard Metrics

The dashboard includes key performance indicators such as:

* Total Sales
* Total Shipments
* Total Boxes
* Total Products
* YoY%

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### 1. Sales by Region and Geography

Analyzes total sales across different regions and countries to identify geographical sales distribution.

### 2. Shipments by Category

Shows the distribution of shipments across major product categories such as:

* Bars
* Bites
* Other

### 3. Shipments by Geography

Provides a geographical breakdown of shipments across countries including the UK, India, USA, Australia, New Zealand and Canada.

### 4. Sales by Product

Compares total sales generated by individual chocolate products and helps identify high- and low-performing products.

### 5. Interactive Filters

The dashboard includes filters for:

* Order Status
* Date

These filters allow users to explore the data based on selected conditions and time periods.
